Save a diagnostic log

If the app crashes, stalls or shows an error, export a log before clearing app data. It includes recent errors, processing stages and device details to help find the cause. Your music and project contents are excluded.

Saved to Downloads → LightForge on Android. If a save dialog opens, choose Downloads. Nothing is sent automatically.

GOOD TO KNOW

The music leads. The car performs.

Every usable channel

Exterior lights, supported fades, cabin RGB, display and the Model 3’s powered closures. Your trim and software determine which fitted lights respond.

Real previews

The perspective preview reads your exported sequence and follows the audio playback position, including when you seek. Front, rear, driver and cabin views show the light groups and estimated window, mirror and trunk travel. The cabin view removes the roof so you can see the display and ambient strips. Tesla sets motor speed and dance strokes; physical movement and perceived brightness can differ from this visual estimate.

Neural rhythm, crafted choreography

Beat This! transformer models analyze rhythm on your device. Choose the full model in Precision or the compact model in Balanced. Voice analysis separates the vocal waveform from the accompaniment, then estimates vocal entrances, notes, accents and releases. Harmonic analysis estimates bass notes; confidence checks keep uncertain detail from taking over. The show engine combines these with beats, energy and musical sections, then checks Tesla’s format and movement limits. No API key or paid service.

Automatic file prep

Audio is converted to 16-bit stereo 44.1 kHz PCM WAV. The export uses uncompressed FSEQ v2 with 200 channels, matching filenames and a supported step time. Your original song is untouched.

Why don’t the lights all fade?

Tesla supports fade commands only on specific channels. Headlights, signals and many exterior lamps are binary. LightForge uses real fades where supported and coordinated patterns elsewhere.

How can I check the voice phrasing?

After analysis, use Full song, Voice and Backing beneath the player to listen to the separated layers at the same moment. Separation can leave some instruments in the voice layer. In Music intelligence, the voice ribbon shows estimated note height and length; jump to a phrase or note, then loop it. If a passage was missed or misidentified, open Correct a voice passage and mark it Voice or Instrumental. These guides shape choreography, stay with the project, and support Undo and Restore analysis.

Listening layers are a local cache. If they are unavailable after restoring a backup or clearing storage, re-analyze the track to create them again. Export always includes your complete song.

What if the detected beat feels wrong?

Open Check & correct the rhythm after creating a show. Try half or double time, choose beats per bar, or pause on a bar’s first beat and mark the playhead. Corrections rebuild the choreography without re-importing music. A small light timing offset in Fine tune can compensate if the actual car looks early or late.

What music files can I use?

Import local WAV, MP3, M4A/MP4 (AAC), AAC, FLAC, OGG, Opus, WebM or MKA audio. Compressed formats use your phone’s available decoder. WAV supports PCM 8/16/24/32-bit and 32-bit float. LightForge automatically prepares 16-bit stereo 44.1 kHz PCM WAV for export; you do not need to convert the song yourself. Extremely long tracks use a lighter mono preview while keeping full-quality stereo exports. Protected streaming downloads and AAC/ADIF files cannot be imported.

Where is my music stored?

Music, analysis and projects stay in the app’s private storage. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting a show removes its stored audio and project. Every exported ZIP also backs up your music, analysis and editing settings. To bring it back, open My shows → Restore backup and choose the original ZIP. Restoring adds a separate project, so existing shows stay intact. Uninstalling the app removes saved projects, so keep a backup ZIP for anything you want to keep.

ON YOUR DEVICE

Work beyond the screen

Creating a show continues when you switch apps, lock the screen, or dismiss the Studio from Recents. Android’s Stop action, Force stop, a restart, memory pressure, or its media-processing time allowance can interrupt a job. Your saved show stays intact; reopen LightForge to retry. Completed analysis checkpoints are reused when the saved inputs still match.
